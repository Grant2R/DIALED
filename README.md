DIALED
Dialed is an app for mountain bikers to encourage them to work on, clean, and fix their bikes.
The users will also use credits that they earn from maintaining their bike to build their next dream bike, sort of like a pet in other apps.
The rider will start of with a "Cluncker" bike with cheap parts, and with their coins they will be able to upgrade the parts on the bike till it becomes their dream ride.
The goal is to help riders manage their rides, and build their next dream mountain bike.

GOALS
Track real bikes and their components.
Record maintenance and service history.
Keep track of component wear and service intervals.
Build and customize virtual dream bikes.
Unlock components and brands through progression.
Create an experience specifically for mountain bikers.
